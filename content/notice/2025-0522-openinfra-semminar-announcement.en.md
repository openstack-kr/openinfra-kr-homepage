### OpenInfra Seminar 2025 May Speaker Recruitment Announcement

We are recruiting speakers for the OpenInfra Seminar 2025. We welcome submissions from anyone interested in presenting on open infrastructure-related topics.

### Overview
**Event Name**: OpenInfra Seminar 2025 May Speaker Recruitment
**Topic**: Cloud utilization cases and all things related to open infrastructure
**Presentation Time**: 40 minutes (including Q&A)
**Presentation Format**: Any format is welcome
**Application Period**: Friday, April 4, 2025 ~ Monday, April 21, 2025

### How to Apply
1. Prepare a presentation proposal.
2. Submit your proposal through the website.

### Review and Selection Process
* Final speakers will be selected after a review of proposals.
