# OpenInfra Days Korea 2025 Ticket Sales Now Open

Under the theme **"The Key to the Future, Open Infrastructure,"** ticket sales have officially started for OpenInfra Days Korea 2025, which will be held on Tuesday, August 26, 2025, at the aT Center (Yangjae), Seoul.

## Key Information

- You can **check the list of speakers** and **purchase tickets** through the official website.  
- The event features various programs including VM Migration, community sessions, and talent recruitment with companies.  
- Not all speaker sessions have been announced yet; the list will be continuously updated.  
- The schedule will be announced separately at a later date.  
- The event will take place on August 26 at the aT Center in Yangjae, Seoul.

## Participating Companies

**Red Hat, Jet Converter Cloud, Ato Research, Kolon Benit, Seagate,** and other leading domestic and international open source IT infrastructure companies will be participating.  
Additionally, various technical contributors will present sessions during the event.

## Ticket Information

- **Price:** 30,000 KRW (including lunch)  
- **Purchase:** [Ticket Purchase Page](https://2025.openinfradays.kr/)  
- **Contact:** support@openinfradays.kr

Please make sure to check your confirmation email after purchasing your ticket.
