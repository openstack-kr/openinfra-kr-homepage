Call for Volunteers: OpenInfra Days Korea 2025

2025-08-10
Author: OpenInfra Korea Team

To ensure the smooth operation of OpenInfra Days Korea 2025, we are looking for volunteers.
Join us in making this open source infrastructure festival a great success!

Recruitment Schedule

  • Application Deadline: August 14, 2025
  • Activity Date: August 26, 2025 (Tue), 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Number of Volunteers: 15

Roles & Responsibilities

  • Assisting with participant registration at the registration booth
  • Supporting session management and facilitation
  • Helping with the Career Zone operation
  • Guiding participants at the venue

Volunteer Benefits

  • Lunch and dinner provided (including after-party participation)
  • Staff T-shirt provided
  • Priority assignment to preferred session duties

Application & Inquiries

  • Application Form: Google Form Link
  • Contact: support@openinfradays.kr

We look forward to your active participation and support!
